AI Architect is a specialist who designs artificial intelligence systems.
They are responsible for model selection, infrastructure, and integration with enterprise systems.
They must understand both business and technology.

RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) is a technique that connects LLMs with custom documents.
It allows the model to answer questions based on specific company data.
It is widely used in enterprise for building chatbots and assistants.

Embeddings are a representation of text as vectors of numbers.
Texts with similar meaning have similar vectors.
This allows us to search documents based on meaning rather than keywords.

Vector store is a database optimized for storing and searching embeddings.
Popular vector stores include ChromaDB, Pinecone, and Azure AI Search.
They enable fast similarity search across thousands of documents.

Chunking is the process of splitting large documents into smaller pieces.
This is necessary because LLMs have a context window limit.
Proper chunk size and overlap are critical for RAG quality.
